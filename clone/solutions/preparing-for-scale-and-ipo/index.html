Build public-company finance before you go public.

Scaling companies can’t rely on spreadsheets, tribal knowledge, or heroic month-end efforts. Nominal’s agents create repeatable, auditable financial workflows that scale with your growth — giving finance leaders the control, visibility, and confidence required for IPO readiness.

Where growth outpaces financial control

Continuous Transaction Monitoring

Agents perpetually reconcile and monitor activity in real-time, ensuring that increasing transaction volumes never introduce risk or close delays.

Automated Control & Audit Trails

Replace manual reviews with automated reconciliations, exception handling, and variance analysis—all backed by a comprehensive, system-generated audit trail.

Autonomous Operational Execution

Agents absorb high-volume operational workloads, offloading manual tasks so finance teams can prioritize reporting, controls, and strategic readiness.

Standardized Global Reporting Framework

Enforce consistent workflows and documentation across all entities, accounts, and periods to meet the highest levels of investor and auditor scrutiny.

Audit-Ready Financial Workflows

Nominal turns informal processes into structured, repeatable systems.

Every reconciliation, match, variance explanation, and follow-up task is logged with full traceability. Finance leaders gain clear evidence of control execution — without manually assembling audit binders or documentation after the fact.

Your close becomes a byproduct of continuous, documented operations, not a scramble to prove accuracy.

Scalable operations without linear hiring

As companies grow, transaction volume and entity complexity increase faster than headcount.

Nominal’s agents handle matching, monitoring, and exception surfacing automatically, allowing finance teams to scale output without scaling manual workload. Instead of hiring just to keep up with reconciliations, teams focus on analysis, controls, and readiness for the next stage of growth.
