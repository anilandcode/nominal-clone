Unlock more from Workday

Workday Financial Management is a powerful system of record. Nominal turns it into a system of execution. By connecting directly to Workday, Nominal’s agents automate reconciliations, monitor variances, and drive close workflows — without changing how your team books transactions.

Fill the Gaps Between Workday and the Close about value adds

  • Automate reconciliations outside the ERP

    Agents continuously match transactions and balances pulled from Workday, eliminating spreadsheet-based recs.

  • Surface issues before month-end

    Anomaly and flux agents detect unusual movements and unmatched activity throughout the period.

  • Reduce manual task coordination

    Trigger Agents automatically initiate investigations and assign follow-ups when issues are detected in Workday data.

  • Turn Workday into a real-time control system

    Instead of periodic review cycles, agents provide continuous monitoring and exception surfacing.

Continuous Reconciliation on Workday

Nominal agents continuously reconcile transactions and balances sourced from Workday. By the time close begins, most routine matching is already complete — leaving your team to focus only on real exceptions.

Real-Time Variance & Exception Monitoring

Flux and anomaly agents analyze Workday balance changes as they happen, drilling into the transactions behind unexpected movements and automatically initiating follow-up
